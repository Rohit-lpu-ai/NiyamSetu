HOW TO USE THIS FILE
--------------------
1. Read data/annotations/README.md first. It is the guideline, and it
   defines what counts as a clause boundary. Do not skip it.
2. This worksheet deliberately does NOT show you the system's output.
   Mark the boundaries you think are correct, from this text and the
   source PDF in data/raw/.
3. For every clause you identify, write one JSONL record as specified
   in the guideline (§4) into:
       data/annotations/segmentation/VER-b8a13c2a7651eb84.annotator2.jsonl
   Set "annotator" to your own name, not person1.
4. Mark "uncertain": true wherever you genuinely cannot decide, and
   say why in "note". Uncertain records are reported separately and
   are more useful than a confident guess.
5. Where the guideline does not answer a case, do NOT invent a rule.
   Record the disagreement; the guideline gets amended (§7).

   4 | Master Circular - Credit Facilities to Scheduled Castes (SCs) & Scheduled Tribes
   5 | (STs)
   6 | 
   7 | Banks should take the measures indicated below to step up their advances to
   8 | SCs/STs.
   9 | 
  10 | 1. Planning Process
  11 | 
  12 | 1.1 The District Level Consultative Committees formed under the Lead Bank Scheme
  13 | should continue to be the principal mechanism of co- ordination between banks and
  14 | development agencies in this regard. The district credit plans formulated by the Lead
  15 | Banks should clearly indicate the linkage of credit with employment and development
  16 | schemes.
  17 | 
  18 | 1.2 Banks will have to establish closer liaison with the District Industries Centres,
  19 | which have been set up in different districts for promoting self-employment.
  20 | 
  21 | 1.3 At the block level, a certain weightage is to be given to SCs/STs in the planning
  22 | process. Accordingly, the credit planning should be weighted in their favour and
  23 | special bankable schemes suited to them should be drawn up to ensure their
  24 | participation and larger flow of credit to them for self-employment. It will be necessary
  25 | for the banks to consider their loan proposals with utmost sympathy and
  26 | understanding.
  27 | 
  28 | 1.4 Banks should periodically review their lending procedures and policies to see that
  29 | loans are sanctioned in time, are adequate and production- oriented and that they
  30 | generate incremental income to make them self-liquidating.
  31 | 
  32 | 1.5 While formulating the Block/ District Credit Plan, special focus may be given to
  33 | villages with sizeable population of SC/ST communities/ specific localities (bastis) in
  34 | the towns/villages having a concentration of these communities.
